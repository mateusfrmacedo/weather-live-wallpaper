# Ícone noturno

Criado com a ferramenta integrada de geração/edição de imagens, a partir do ícone anterior do aplicativo (Tempo espelhado). Arquivo final: AppIcon-Night.png. Os tamanhos de 16 a 1024 pixels são produzidos por Scripts/make-icon.m.

Prompt inicial: preserve the Apple Weather-style rounded-square silhouette, padding, luminous bevel and glassy cloud composition; replace the upper-left yellow sun with a pale ivory crescent moon; remove yellow light from the cloud; change the tile to midnight navy; preserve transparency outside; no stars, text or added elements.

Prompt final: Precise production icon cleanup edit of this macOS nighttime weather icon. Preserve exactly the crescent moon and foreground cloud shapes, positions, glass material, overall rounded-square tile shape and margins. Make the tile background a MUCH DARKER midnight navy smooth uniform gradient (top #08142f, bottom #142f62). No mottling or cloudy patches in the background. The tile rim must be subtle navy blue, not bright electric cyan. Critical alpha cleanup: remove ALL blue/cyan speckles and stray pixels outside the rounded-square tile. Exterior must be perfectly clean fully transparent alpha zero, a smooth antialiased tile boundary only. No glow bleeding past the tile, no exterior shadow, no scattered pixels, no stars, no text. Keep the pale ivory crescent and cool pale blue-white cloud polished and crisp. This is the final icon bitmap, not a mockup.

A imagem-fonte final já está espelhada, com a Lua à direita. O gerador de iconset apenas redimensiona essa fonte, sem aplicar uma segunda inversão.
